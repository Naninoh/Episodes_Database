> **Special Features:** Queen's Big Fing Club is an [[Excellent Quality]] [[Exotic Weapon|Exotic]] Very Heavy Melee Weapon.
> 
> It is a large Blunt Club like item with three modes of use: Neutral, Puncture, and Shred modes. It also has a Biometric Lock that forbids anyone but the owner from using with hand-eviscerating effects to any who tries. Modes can be chosen at the start of an attack action. 
> 
> Neutral Mode: Simply a big hunk of Metal. No other effects than the listed above stats. 
> 
> Puncture Mode: Specializing in dealing with Armored Targets. Puncture Mode can only be used for Aimed Attacks. While Active, Armor 11 or Under is ignored. 
> 
> Shred Mode: Specializing for Crowd Control, per user's wish this can make the weapon either deal Damage 3d6+1 and be RoF2, or allow a 4d6 damage hit against up to all targets in melee range with the user rolling their attack once.
