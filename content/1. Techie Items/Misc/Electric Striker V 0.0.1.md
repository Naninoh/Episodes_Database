> **Special Features:** The Electric Striker requires an Interface Plug to be plugged in for use. While active, it is a weapon system with multiple profiles that uses 'Radline Magna Slot Battery Bricks' (100eb) to power the following functions while providing a total of 32 charges: 
> 
> **(4 Charges):** It can be a Non-Lethal Shotgun that 'fires' a **4d6** damage Shotgun Shell of lightning. This does not ablate armor and when a target is reduced below 1 HP, their HP is set to 1 and they are rendered [[unconscious]]. 
> 
> **(16 Charges):** During a grapple, and specifically during a choke action, you may expend charges to force the target to take a DV13 [[Resist Torture or Drugs]] check. If failed, the target is rendered [[unconscious]] for 1 minute. 
> 
> **(8 Charges):** attacks done with this weapon: Targets must take a DV 15 [[Cybertech]] check or suffer an EMP effect. (GM chooses two pieces of Cyberware to fail).
