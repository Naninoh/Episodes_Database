> **Special Features:** The Concealed Blade Vambrace is a gauntlet that has within it a wrist-mounted blade that at all times can be considered concealed and will pass all checks unless a character is directly aware of the Concealed Blade Vambraces' function before hand. 
> 
> The Vambrace has an [[Excellent Quality]] Light Melee Weapon inside the wrist-housing, that can be deployed 'At will' with a motion of the hand. 
> 
> While a character is wearing the Vambrace, they may take a [[Stealth]] Check as an Action (or as an incidental at start of combat) to use for the Vambrace. Whenever that character spends any portion of their turn within Melee Range of a target, the Target rolls a Perception Check against the earlier [[Stealth]] Check: On fail, the target has been 'pricked' without noticing it, and suffers 1d6 damage ignoring armor. 
> 
> The 'Draining' aspect comes in the form of whenever damage is dealt with this weapon against anyone that can get meat-hit, (Non FBCs, Robots) the Draining Gauntlet will fill one of 3 Vials it has. Each vial will be loaded with the blood of the target. 
> 
> When being used as an active melee weapon, the Vambrace does not confer the stealth check as above and does not ignore armor.
