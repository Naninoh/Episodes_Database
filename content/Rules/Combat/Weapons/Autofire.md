When you use Autofire, **it costs an Action and 10 bullets**. If you don't have 10 bullets remaining in your clip, you can't use Autofire. 

**You use the Autofire Skill** instead of the weapon's typical Weapon Skill. Additionally, instead of the weapon's entry on the Range Table, you use its entry on the [[#Autofire Range Table]]. 

Autofire cannot be used to make an Aimed Shot. **Targets with REF 8 or Higher can still choose to attempt to dodge your Autofire.** 

> [!important]
> If you hit, **roll 2d6 for damage**, and **multiply it by the amount you beat the DV to hit your target**, up to a maximum denoted by the weapon's Autofire (3 for SMGS, 4 for Assault Rifles). This number is the amount of damage that Autofire dealt. 
> - Example: The Target decided to dodge and came up with a 13. You hit them with a 19. Your Autofire is 4.  $2D6 * 4 = Damage$

- If both dice came up 6, you've also inflicted a Critical Injury! 
- The Defenders [[Armor|armor reduces the damage]] you do!

#### Autofire Range Table
DV based on distance:

|     Weapon Type     | 0 to 6m | 7 to 12m | 13 to 25m | 26 to 50m | 51 to 100m |

|      **SMGs:**      |   20    |    17    |    20     |    25     |     30     |
| **Assault Rifles:** |   22    |    20    |    17     |    20     |     25     |
