**Poor Quality Weapons malfunction whenever you roll a Critical Failure (a 1 on your Attack Check),** requiring you to use an Action to reverse the malfunction before they can be used again. 

*Unjamming your weapon doesn't require a Check.* Cost varies based on the weapon’s base cost.
