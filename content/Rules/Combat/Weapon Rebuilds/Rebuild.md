*A Rebuild is a type of attachment that can convert a Cyberpunk RED firearm into a [[Power Rebuild|Power]], [[Smart Rebuild|Smart]], or [[Tech Rebuild|Tech]] Weapon. Installing or uninstalling a Rebuild takes 1 hour. A Rebuild can be installed in an Exotic Ranged Weapon in a single attachment slot if one is available. Only one Rebuild can be installed in a single weapon.*
