The DV and amount of time it takes you to repair a damaged/destroyed item with a [[Technique (TECH)]] Skill to perfect condition is based on the Price Category of the item:

|      *Cost*      | *Difficulty Value (DV)* |           *Time to repair*           |
| :------------: | :-------------------: | :--------------------------------: |
|     *Costly*     |         *DV13*          |              *6 Hours*               |
|    *Premium*     |         *DV17*          |               *1 Day*                |
|   *Expensive*    |         *DV21*          |               *1 Week*               |
| *Very Expensive* |         *DV24*          |              *2 Weeks*               |
|     *Luxury*     |         *DV29*          |              *1 Month*               |
|  *Super Luxury*  |         *DV29*          | *1 Month per 10,000eb of Item Price* |

*On a failed roll, halfway through the repair, you realize that you'll have to start the repair from scratch.*
