The DV and amount of time it takes you to repair a damaged/destroyed vehicle with a [[Technique (TECH)]] Skill to perfect condition is based on the amount of damage it has sustained. 

|    *Damage*    | *Difficulty Value (DV)* | *Time to repair* |
| :------------: | :---------------------: | :--------------: |
| *Minor Damage* |         *DV 9*          |    *3 Hours*     |
| *Major Damage* |         *DV13*          |     *1 Day*      |
|  *Destroyed*   |         *DV17*          |     *1 Week*     |

*On a failed roll, halfway through the repair, you realize that you'll have to start the repair from scratch.*
