Stat: [[Technique (TECH)]]

> [!Success] Skill of repairing and maintaining vehicles that move on land. [[Vehicle Repair|The DV and amount of time]] it takes you to repair a damaged/destroyed vehicle with this Skill to perfect condition is based on the amount of damage it has sustained.

---
At a Base 10, you have gotten good at maintaining and repairing roadbikes, groundcars, and other land vehicles. 

At a Base 14, you are a skilled mechanic, who can easily repair almost any damage to a land vehicle given enough time. 

At a Base 18, you can salvage and rebuild old military transports from the badlands and you could fix a flat while in your sleep.
