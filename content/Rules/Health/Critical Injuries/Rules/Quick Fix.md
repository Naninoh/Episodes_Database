> [!attention] Effect:
> A Quick Fix will remove the Injury Effect for the **rest of the day**.
> 
> Each attempt takes a minute. You **can** Quick Fix yourself.

If you suffer a Critical Injury to a section of your body that has been replaced with Cyberware you still suffer the Effect of the Critical Injury and its Bonus Damage. 

However, you can use the [[Cybertech]] Skill to perform a Quick Fix in place of the [[First Aid]], [[Paramedic]], or [[Surgery]] Skills. *This Check is made at the listed DV and takes the same amount of time.*
