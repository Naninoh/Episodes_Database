> [!attention] Effect:
> A Treatment will remove the Injury Effect **permanently**. 
> 
> Each attempt takes **four hours**. You **can't** do a Treatment on yourself.

If you suffer a Critical Injury to a section of your body that has been replaced with Cyberware you still suffer the Effect of the Critical Injury and its Bonus Damage. 

However, you can use the [[Cybertech]] Skill to perform a Treatment in place of the [[First Aid]], [[Paramedic]], or [[Surgery]] Skills. *This Check is made at the listed DV and takes the same amount of time.*
