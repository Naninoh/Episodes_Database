
> [!failure] Mortally Wounded
> - **Threshold**: Less than One HP
> 
> - **Wound Effect**: 
> 	- **-4 to all Actions** 
> 	  
> 	- **-6 to MOVE** (Minimum 1) 
> 	  
> 	- Must make a [[Death Save]] at start of each one of their Turns. 
> 	  
> 	- Mortally Wounded Characters suffer a [[Critical Injuries Rules#Critical Injury Table|Critical Injury]] whenever they are damaged by a Melee or Ranged Attack. 
> 		- **In addition, their Death Save Penalty increases by 1.**
>   
> - **[[Stabilization]] DV**: 15
