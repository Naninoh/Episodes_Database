Assuming you don't end up dead, you'll want to heal up so you can get back on The Street and do it all over again. To begin the natural healing process, you must first receive stabilization.

<p align="center"> The DV to Stabilize a target (or yourself) depends on what Wound State your target is in.
<br>
<br>
<b>Lightly Wounded: DV10
<br><br>
Seriously Wounded: DV13
<br><br>
Mortally Wounded: DV15</b>
<br>
</p>

Stabilization takes an Action and is rolled: **[[Technique (TECH)|TECH]] + [[First Aid]] Skill or [[Paramedic]] Skill + 1d10.**
